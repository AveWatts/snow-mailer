HOW TO READ THIS
Condition: 5 powder (6 in or more new in 3 days, no crust), 4 packed or settled powder (new snow in 7 days or groomed in 48 hours), 3 packed, firm or spring corn, 2 thin cover or crust (rain, freeze-thaw or wind since the last snow), 1 too thin to ski. Skiable from {{ skiable_depth.backcountry }} in backcountry, {{ skiable_depth.nordic }} in nordic, {{ skiable_depth.downhill }} in downhill; below {{ good_depth.backcountry }} / {{ good_depth.nordic }} / {{ good_depth.downhill }} in, condition is capped at 2.
Each section lists the most promising areas first: open, then sketchy, then closed; within each, condition, then depth and fresh snow.
Best bets list open areas before sketchy ones, and rank each by snow depth on Sunday (counted up to 120 in) and fresh snow (the last 3 days plus forecast snowfall through Sunday, counted up to 24 in), weighted equally.
Snow level is the elevation where rain turns to snow (NBM, lowest to highest over the period). ^ means it reaches above the area.
Temperatures are high / low from the NWS forecast, adjusted from the forecast grid's elevation to the area's at 3.5F per 1,000 ft. Now is today's high and tonight's low; weekend is the warmest Saturday or Sunday high and the coldest Saturday or Sunday morning low.
Status: downhill areas are open or closed as the resort reports it. Nordic centers with a grooming report (Meadows Nordic and the Nordic Pulse areas) are open when groomed in the last 7 days. Other sno-parks and backcountry follow the condition: 1 closed, 2 sketchy, 3 or better open; a road closure closes the areas behind it. Weekend confidence drops when the NBM 10th-90th percentile precipitation range is wide, when the snow level is within 1,000 ft of the area, or when the NWS forecast disagrees with NBM.
{% if has_drive %}Drive times are from your base location in normal road conditions (OpenStreetMap routing); chains, closures and traffic can add time. Summits and the Muir Snowfield route to their winter access point.
